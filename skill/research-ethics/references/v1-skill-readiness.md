# research-ethics V1 skill readiness gate

This file prevents an evidence-preview rule package from being mistaken for a
production skill. `registration-tree.yaml` remains the only platform-rule
source; the ledger records what has actually been sampled.

## Already ready

- Researcher-initiated V1 scope is explicit; all product-registration routes
  are `deferred_to_v2`.
- Canonical conditions, field paths, option references, and generated
  candidate scenarios pass local structural validation.
- The evidence ledger distinguishes current-page checks, downstream spot
  checks, inferred nodes, and mismatches.
- A non-rejoining evidence preview can be mechanically regenerated without
  retaining screenshots, source, credentials, or study values.
- The zero-leaf unmerged-tree scaffold is explicitly detected as incomplete;
  it must not be presented as the final root-to-leaf tree. The V1 HTML
  artifact is therefore an evidence preview until completed display routes
  exist.

## V1 generation boundary

The unresolved `unproduct > other` mismatch was deliberately stopped at the
user's direction. It is therefore `out_of_scope_or_blocked`, has a local
protocol, and is excluded from supported V1 generation. A filling draft must
show an explicit unsupported-case notice instead of guessing its downstream
fields.

The following evidence limits remain visible in the V1 skill. They are not
license to invent fields or values:

1. Obtain representative evidence for the high-risk blind/allocation and
   multicenter child conditions, or explicitly suppress their conditional
   content in generated filling instructions.
2. Confirm at least one safe representative path for every page included in
   the user-facing sequence, especially the observational diagnostic-no
   attachment route and the interventional recruitment/attachment path.
3. Verify repeat-template semantics for every repeated group exposed by the
   skill, not merely the base template.
4. Promote only rules with an adequate evidence grade to generation. Rules
   marked `inferred_from_initial_tree`, `mismatch_found`, or
   `out_of_scope_or_blocked` must become a user confirmation or an explicit
   unsupported-case notice; they must never be silently filled. A
   `verification_by_route` override may permit a separately sampled V1 route
   while retaining a mismatch on a deferred V2 route.

The observed `unproduct > other` difference remains in the ledger as a
documented exclusion rather than being promoted into the canonical condition
model. The model must not absorb a current-page-only correction until the
affected branch has enough local evidence and its generated condition
scenarios remain internally coverable.

The exact non-persistent local verification steps for this branch are in
`unproduct-other-local-dfs-protocol.md`.

## Build trigger

The gate now permits a V1 skill with explicit exclusions. The skill must
consume the canonical YAML plus the evidence status, ask only for values that
cannot be derived from the research plan, and produce a sequential Chinese
copy/paste checklist. It must stop rather than silently generate the excluded
subroute.
